# TikTok app review explanation — APPROVED by Rishi 2026-11-04 (not yet submitted to TikTok)

## Chronicle & Chaos (565 chars)

Chronicle & Chaos is an educational channel publishing short mythology and history videos. Login Kit (user.info.basic) lets the channel owner securely sign in to TikTok and confirm their identity before publishing. Content Posting API (video.upload) creates a draft in the TikTok inbox for review, and (video.publish) posts the approved vertical video directly to the channel after the owner approves it in our uploader, with the title, caption and privacy setting shown before posting. No video is posted without owner approval, and no other user data is accessed.
